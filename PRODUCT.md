# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Privacy-sovereignty seekers. They arrive not holding XFG, and they arrive skeptical: privacy coins are a category littered with abandoned promises, and this visitor has been disappointed before. Their job here is to decide whether Fuego is the exception and then go get the wallet. They read the specs table for proof, they check claims against the source repository if anything feels convenient, and they decide within one visit. They are technically fluent and allergic to being sold to.

## Product Purpose

Fuego (XFG) is a CryptoNote privacy coin presented as a private reserve: ring signatures, a fixed supply, no premine, no inflation schedule. The site exists to make a skeptical technical visitor trust a scarce asset, and to hand them off to the wallet or the source. Success is a visitor who came in distrusting and leaves convinced, then installs. Failure is a visitor who leaves because the page performed confidence instead of earning it.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, scarce, enduring. Declarative voice: statements, not persuasion. The first five seconds should read as composure, not as welcome. Urgency, exclamation, badges, and social proof are all off-register. Luxury expressed through subtraction and through material weight, never through ornament.

## Anti-references

- **Crypto-native neon.** Black backgrounds with laser gradients, token-ticker confetti, meme energy, coin-speculation aesthetics.
- **Corporate fintech.** Navy-and-gold trust landing pages, badge strips, card product imagery, the visual language of a brokerage.
- **AI-slop glass.** Frosted translucent panels, gradient text, purple-to-blue SaaS heroes, glowing orbs used as decoration.

## Design Principles

1. **Subtract until it breaks.** Scarcity is the argument, so the page argues by removal. Every element must survive the question "what does this add?"; when in doubt, cut it rather than style it.
2. **Ship only what traces to source.** Every number and capability claim must be verifiable in the fuego-suite source tree or explicitly specified by the owner. When a claim cannot be verified, remove it rather than hedge it.
3. **Meet skepticism with statements.** The audience arrives distrusting. Plain declarative sentences and checkable specifics build credibility; persuasion mechanics destroy it.
4. **Aesthetic restraint serves permanence.** Nothing should look like it was made this year. Materials and type age better than trends, which is what "enduring" has to mean in practice.
5. **Privacy is claimed, so the page must not undercut it.** No surveillance-shaped analytics, no attention-grabbing motion theater, no borrowed visual language from the advertising category the brand positions against.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG level is claimed. Commitments nonetheless in force:

- `prefers-reduced-motion` is honored sitewide, including the WebGL starfield and all scroll and pointer parallax.
- Focus states stay visible; the skip link is the first focusable element.
- Small uppercase labels sit at the bottom of the contrast range and must be measured before use, not assumed. Gold-dim on obsidian is the known risk area.
- No meaning is carried by color alone; chain identity always has a text label beside it.
