Privacy

Dynamic Mixin

Ring signatures with dynamic decoy counts (min 8 | 16 | 32 max). Each transaction mixes with a changing decoy set.

Stealth Sub-Addresses

One-time subaddresses for every transaction. Receive funds at a fresh address each time without linking your master address.

Adaptor Signatures

Privacy-preserving cross-chain exchange. Unlike HTLCs, adaptor signatures publish no revealing hashes on-chain.

Dandelion++

Network-level privacy that obfuscates the originating IP of a transaction, preventing geographic or network-based analysis.
