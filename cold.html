Current architecture / HEAT term CDs

Two movements. One deposit discipline.

The current atelier first transforms XFG into HEAT through a permanent burn priced by the Hearth pool. HEAT may then be placed into an epoch-locked certificate whose interest is funded by protocol fee pools.

Supersession notice

COLD is not the live deposit protocol.

The COLD certificate described in the archived Wightpaper has been superseded by the HEAT-based term CD. The earlier STARK deposit design is deprecated. The current code has also removed Ember_Heat and direct XFG deposits. This notice follows DEPOSIT_ARCHITECTURE.md, Current vs Deprecated Systems in the Fuego source architecture.

Deposit sequence

The current two-step caliber.

A term CD never accepts XFG directly. Minting and deposit are separate operations with separate standing.

Step 01 / Mint

Burn XFG through mintHeatV10()

XFG is permanently burned. The HEAT amount is priced from the Hearth pool time-weighted average, with the current pool rate used when that average is unavailable. There is no fixed HEAT-per-XFG conversion ratio.

Step 02 / Lock

Place HEAT into a term CD

Already-minted HEAT is locked for an allowed epoch term. A commitment output records the term, while the locally held deposit secret preserves the authority required for later redemption or rollover.

Distinction / 01

Minting is not a certificate

The burn has no term and earns no interest. It is the irreversible transformation into the HEAT complication. Only the second movement creates an epoch-locked CD.

Operational record

Three views of the same instrument.

These panels are documentation, not wallet controls. Each view is available by keyboard and requires no script.

First movement

Mint HEAT

The wallet calls mintHeatV10(), selects XFG inputs for the burn and fee, and creates HEAT commitment outputs at the Hearth pool TWAP price.

Second movement

Lock the term

The wallet calls createDeposit() with HEAT principal and one allowed term. The commitment output itself carries the term record.

Maturity / Spend

Commitment output, ring-signature spend

At maturity, the wallet calculates available interest from the CD pool and spends the old commitment output with a ring signature. Redemption returns principal with available interest. Rollover creates a new commitment output for the newly selected term.

Local secret Ring signature Maturity bound

Fee routing

Interest follows collected fees

A share of epoch swap fees is routed to the CD yield pool. This is a fee-routing rule, not a voting or consensus threshold.
